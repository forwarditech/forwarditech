Our Story

Nigeria's Premier
ICT Solutions Provider

Forward I-Tech Solutions Investment Ltd is a CAC-registered, privately held technology company headquartered in Bauchi, Nigeria. Since our founding in July 2018, we have delivered comprehensive ICT services — from custom software systems to satellite internet infrastructure — enabling businesses, schools, farms, and hospitals to operate smarter and more efficiently.

We combine deep technical expertise with local knowledge to provide solutions that are both world-class in quality and tailored to the Nigerian market. Our multi-disciplinary team handles the full technology stack — from silicon to software, from satellite dish to digital dashboard.
